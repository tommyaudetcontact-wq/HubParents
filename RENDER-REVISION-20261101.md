# 3D-0.21 render correction

The ground painter is baked into disjoint convex polygons during mesh construction for each cached terrain revision. This keeps the original colour order, including the octagonal circuit and its markings, without coplanar road/earth surfaces fighting for depth. Ramps and raised geometry remain independent. No texture atlas or per-frame clipping is introduced. The generic decal layer resolver no longer collapses ranks above 32.

Walking retains a 0.7-unit knee reach margin to avoid the near-straight IK singularity. The maximum measured bone angular velocity for six seconds at pedestrian speed remains below 4 rad/s for male/female rigs at 30/60/120 Hz with a 10 Hz source phase. NPC displayed velocity is filtered and is no longer overwritten by a second finite-difference estimate before gait sampling. Host simulation, collision positions and player movement rules are unchanged.

Eight previously simplified vehicles now use native road geometry: Riviera, Azur, Rosalie, Rallye, Colosse, Titan, Routier and Chantier. Convertibles have open cockpits without floating rear glass or quarter-window frames. Azur has two seats and a rear tonneau. Rallye has auxiliary lamps, stripes and a supported spoiler. Monsters have thicker tires, raised bodies, axles and suspension. Routier has a ribbed cargo box and a rear shutter attached to its damage panel. Cabin anchors, wheel animation and native damage/debris use the existing adapter. These are procedural family variants, not completed fidelity passes for the five pending luxury/muscle references.

Validation: city-three-ground-tests.cjs checks 12,000 world samples against painter order, no overlapping tile interiors and retained ramps; city-three-pedestrian-gait-tests.cjs samples both skeletons; city-three-npc-motion-tests.cjs checks source/display timing; city-three-road-variants-tests.cjs checks variant structure; city-three-road-model-tests.cjs checks all 27 routed entries for deformation uploads, finite geometry, wheel movement and exact repair. Existing stability, motion, road handling and debris-network tests pass. Browser QA uses a disposable local scene with continuous and 10 Hz pedestrians; no account data changed. Real Android performance and appearance still need device validation.
