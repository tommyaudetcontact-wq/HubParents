# Fleet reconstruction — observed reference design

The approved yellow coupe is the style reference, not the replacement shape for every car. The new multi-view sheets depict original game vehicles, without real brand identity.

## Sedan observation
- Bilaterally symmetric three-box four-door body. The wheelbase occupies about 63% of body length; the cabin is lower than the previous generic generator. A broad hood and separate trunk visibly close the front and rear volumes.
- Continuous crowned hood, swollen fender shoulders and a single rising belt crease. Arches are cut openings with a narrow lip, dark wells and tire clearance, not painted rectangles over tires.
- Four rectangular headlamps, a recessed black horizontal grille, integrated silver bumper, two amber indicators, rear rectangular red/amber lamps and license recess.
- Transparent lightly blue glazing follows the windshield, roof and door perimeters. Door seams, handles and B pillars are flush. The main pane and frame follow a detached door; the fixed quarter pane stays on the body.
- Five-spoke wheels are recessed within the tire sidewalls. Seat bases, backs, headrests, dashboard, wheel and inner liners form a closed inhabited cabin.
- Inference: hidden engine-bay walls, trunk liner, floor pan, door thickness and wheel-well barrels must close the unseen volume. These use restrained original geometry, not a claim of exact unseen reference reconstruction.

## Additional family observations
- Compact: two side doors, short hood, steep hatch, continuous rounded roof and rear quarter glass. Circular headlamps and graphite multi-spoke wheels distinguish it from the four-door sedan.
- SUV: taller five-door cabin, longer roof, rear quarter glazing, roof rails, dark lower cladding and larger arch clearance. Four round lamps retain the approved family language.
- Pickup: four-door cab with upright rear window and separate open lined cargo bed. Raised chassis and tires require full inner wheel tubs and visible axle structure.

## Quality contract before modeling
Six primary reference families plus derived wagon, electric, convertible, minivan, monster and delivery variants. Vehicle silhouettes must remain distinct. No common cuboid passenger cage. Macro: lower shell, upper cabin, four wheels, hood/trunk and doors. Meso: true arches/wells, optical recesses, transparent windows, interior, bumper and seams. Micro: handles, mirrors on attached stalks, grille slats, headlamp inset, wheel hubs, tire shoulder rings, rear plate and cargo/roof details. Flat paint uses solid albedo; no photo projection or invented branding.

Maximum 28k triangles and 45 draw calls per close vehicle after batches; distant traffic retains the existing LOD. Damage cannot hide an empty engine bay, black protruding box or floating sill. Detached parts retain the exact model, glass/frame, original paint and existing physical lifetime. Wheels spin and steer. Driver placement and networked damage state remain compatible.

Review front, rear, both sides and a three-quarter view. Block on wrong body class, slab/cage cabin, floating trim, rim outside rubber, body across an open arch, mismatched glass, unclosed cavity or nonfunctional damage hierarchy. Generated references are stylized design guides, not extracted meshes.
